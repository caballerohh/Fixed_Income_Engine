# Fixed Income Risk Analytics

A Python-based framework for corporate bond valuation, interest-rate risk analysis, credit-spread assessment and scenario stress testing.

The project separates the major drivers of fixed-income performance to determine whether changes in value arise primarily from rates, credit conditions or carry.

## Analytical Capabilities

- Bond pricing and yield-to-maturity estimation
- Macaulay and modified duration
- Convexity and non-linear price sensitivity
- Treasury-curve and credit-spread analysis
- Yield and OAS decomposition
- Historical return attribution
- Scenario and stress testing
- Automated credit tear-sheet generation

## Repository Purpose

The framework is designed to connect bond mathematics with portfolio and credit decisions. Its inputs can be adapted to analyze different issuers, instruments and market scenarios while maintaining a consistent reporting structure.

Specific instruments, market assumptions and analysis dates may evolve as the automated reporting framework is standardized.
